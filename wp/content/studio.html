— Our Team

An independent studio.
Four disciplines under one roof.

Lucid is an independent design studio — four disciplines under one roof, run personally by four Founding Partners. We operate between the EU and the GCC: project delivery from our hub in Warsaw, Poland, and on-the-ground presence in Abu Dhabi, with partners in Riyadh, Dubai and Muscat.

We treat each commission as architecture. Lighting, signage & wayfinding, urban custom furniture, and art and digital placemaking — each a discipline in its own right, coordinated by a single lead architect under one shared visual language.

— Core Values

Creativity
