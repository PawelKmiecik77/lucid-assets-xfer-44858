— Wayfinding & Signage

WayDrafter™

Wayfinding & Signage Design Software

Project as a data system — guided from concept analysis to controlled handover.

WayDrafter is our internal environment for designing Wayfinding & Signage systems. It connects spatial analysis, user scenarios, content programming, carrier logic and documentation control into one structured process — reducing repetitive work, limiting coordination errors and keeping decisions, sign locations, message content and final documentation consistent.

— Urban Furniture Consulting

Urban Furniture Simulator

Urban Elements Placement Software

Placement as evidence — grounded in simulation, not in guesswork.

A simulation-driven environment for planning urban furniture — seating, shade and amenities across plazas, parks, concourses and public realms. An agent-based crowd model built on the validated Collision-Free Speed model, with our own algorithms for destination choice, dwell time, comfort and the behavioural weight of greenery, turns pedestrian behaviour into measurable placement intelligence.
