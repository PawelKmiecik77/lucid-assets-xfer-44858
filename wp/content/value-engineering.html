— Approach

Value engineering as a design discipline.

Six principles that guide how Lucid evaluates and protects spatial design under project constraints.

— 01

Protect design intent

Value engineering is approached as a design discipline. The goal is not to strip the project down, but to maintain its design intent under cost, lifecycle and buildability pressure.

— 02

Reduce coordination risk

Earlier coordination decisions, made within a BIM-led environment, reduce downstream change cost and protect the integrity of the spatial system.

— 03

Compare equivalent options

We compare technically equivalent options — products, materials, fixings, control logic — using clear evaluation criteria rather than abstract preference.

— 04

Balance CAPEX and OPEX

Decisions consider lifecycle performance, maintenance, energy and operational impact, not only initial investment cost.

— 05

Document decisions

Each value engineering decision is documented with rationale, ensuring that the project record reflects intent, not only the final outcome.

— 06

Support procurement and implementation

Output is structured for procurement, contractor onboarding and site implementation — supporting the project rather than complicating it.
