Architects of Light

Independent Architectural & Urban Lighting Consulting

Lucid is an independent design studio specialising in architectural and urban lighting. We combine aesthetic sensitivity with engineering precision to integrate light with architecture and landscape — not a luminaire supplier and not a generic consultancy.

The work is BIM-first. We model lighting in Revit, run photometric simulations through Revit-integrated plugins or standalone tools such as DIALux and Relux, coordinate against architectural and MEP models, and — where a project requires a luminaire that does not exist on the market — we engineer it: IFC, RFA and STEP, fabrication-ready.

— FAQ

Frequently asked.

Common questions about scope, deliverables and coordination.

— 01Do you provide lighting calculations and simulations?+
Yes. Each project is supported by photometric calculations and visual-comfort analysis, run in Revit-integrated plugins or standalone tools such as DIALux and Relux, scaled to the complexity of the environment.
— 02Can you engineer a luminaire that does not exist on the market?+
Yes. Where a project requires bespoke hardware, we engineer it and deliver IFC, RFA and STEP files, fabrication-ready, inside the same BIM workflow as the rest of the design.
— 03Can lighting be coordinated with signage, furniture, art and digital layers?+
Yes. Our BIM-led workflow allows lighting to be developed in coordination with the other Lucid disciplines as one federated spatial system.
— 04Do you design intelligent lighting control and support green-building standards?+
Yes. We specify intelligent lighting control systems such as DALI and KNX, and our energy and technical analysis supports green-building certification targets including LEED, BREEAM and WELL.
