Architects of the Way

Independent Signage & Wayfinding Consulting

Lucid is a signage and wayfinding consulting practice — not a signage supplier and not a graphic studio. We work on the layer of decisions behind the visible system: topology studies, signage matrix programmes, carrier engineering, BIM family creation, tender documentation and optional site supervision.

The work is BIM-first. We model carriers in Revit, create our own BIM families when manufacturer files are unavailable, distribute them into the client’s model and prepare STEP / IFC / RFA artefacts for coordination and fabrication. Bilingual and bidirectional layouts (EN / AR), heritage-context constraints and GCC cultural literacy are part of the discipline, not an afterthought.

— Principles

Wayfinding is decision architecture.

Routes before signs. Logic before medium.

— 01

Decision before sign

Every spatial node is first analysed as a user decision point. The sign appears at the end of the process — as an answer, not as the starting point.

— 02

User perspective

Every decision is checked from the perspective of a user entering the space for the first time: without knowledge of the building, organisational shortcuts or the designer’s intent.

— 03

System over object

We design information architecture, not individual boards. Each carrier is part of a system: routes, decisions, messages and delivery standards.

— FAQ

Frequently asked.

Common questions about scope, deliverables and coordination.

— 01What is included in a signage and wayfinding strategy?+
Spatial and topology analysis, user flow logic, decision-point mapping, signage hierarchy, the signage matrix programme and a typology system that informs every subsequent design and documentation stage.
— 02Do you provide BIM-ready signage documentation?+
Yes. We produce Revit-ready RFA families, STEP documentation (ISO 10303) and BIM-coordinated sign distribution as part of our standard delivery scope.
— 03Do you handle bilingual EN / AR and heritage-context signage?+
Yes. Bilingual and bidirectional layouts, heritage-context constraints and GCC cultural literacy are treated as part of the discipline, not an afterthought.
— 04What does the client receive at handover?+
A Sign Book, RFA families and BIM model, 2D production files (DWG / DXF), STEP files for custom carriers, a BOQ extracted from the model, placement plans and a QA / compliance report.
