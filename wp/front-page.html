— FAQ

Frequently asked.

How Lucid is engaged, where it works, and what BIM-led delivery means.

— 01Can we commission a single discipline, or is the full package required?+
Either. Each of the four Lucid disciplines can be commissioned independently or as one integrated package — coordinated by a single lead architect inside one BIM model.
— 02Where does Lucid deliver projects?+
Between Europe and the GCC — project delivery from a Warsaw hub, an Abu Dhabi office, and partners in Riyadh, Dubai and Muscat.
— 03What does BIM-led delivery mean?+
Every discipline is designed as structured data inside one federated Revit model, so design intent is preserved from concept through to fabrication-ready documentation — RFA families, STEP (ISO 10303) and IFC.
— 04What are WayDrafter and the Urban Furniture Simulator?+
They are Lucid's proprietary tools. WayDrafter designs wayfinding & signage as data; the Urban Furniture Simulator places urban elements using agent-based pedestrian simulation (the Collision-Free Speed model). Both feed the same BIM model.
